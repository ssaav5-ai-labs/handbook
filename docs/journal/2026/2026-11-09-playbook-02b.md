### Step 8 - Join k3s-03 as a server (and the pve03 NIC hang)
PR [homelab-infra#10](https://github.com/ssaav5-ai-labs/homelab-infra/pull/10): added `k3s-03 server` to `nodes.conf`. First install attempt hit a cert SAN issue (k3s-01's cert lacked the VIP SAN before regenerating it). After deleting `serving-kube-apiserver.crt`/`.key`/`dynamic-cert.json` and restarting k3s-01, the cert included `192.168.86.210`.

The second attempt exposed a `pve03` NIC problem: the e1000e driver logged `Detected Hardware Unit Hang`, the link dropped, and etcd lost quorum (k3s-03 runs on `pve03`). Labelled `homelab.io/size=large`. Cluster now has 2 etcd members (no failure tolerance); must proceed to steps 9 and 10 in same sitting.

### Step 12 - Failure drill and the single-replica gap
Drill (🧑): `qm shutdown 101` (k3s-01) at ~16:13, `qm start 101` at ~16:19. The control plane survived — `kubectl` and `kubectl -n argocd get applications` answered through the VIP, and `k3s-01` went `NotReady`. But ingress and DNS were both down for ~5 minutes: Traefik and CoreDNS each ran a single replica on `k3s-01`, and nothing answered until the kubelet's pod eviction rescheduled Traefik → `k3s-03` and CoreDNS → `k3s-02`. All three nodes came back `Ready` after restart. So losing one node no longer breaks `kubectl`, but still broke the gateway and DNS for the eviction window.

Fix — Traefik: added `k3s/traefik/helmchartconfig.yaml`, a `HelmChartConfig` (Traefik is a k3s HelmChart) setting `deployment.replicas: 2` plus a `requiredDuringScheduling` pod anti-affinity on `kubernetes.io/hostname`. Wired into `install.sh` (server-only, same pattern as kube-vip) and applied live: 2 pods on `k3s-01`/`k3s-02`, anti-affinity confirmed on the Deployment.

Fix — CoreDNS: CoreDNS is **not** a HelmChart (only `traefik`/`traefik-crd` are) and has no `HelmChartConfig` hook, so instead we scaled the embedded deployment to 2. Empirical test: `kubectl -n kube-system scale deploy coredns --replicas=2`, then restarted k3s on `k3s-02` and `k3s-03` one at a time — the scale **sticks** (k3s does not revert the replicas field), and the two pods landed on `k3s-01`/`k3s-02`. `install.sh` now re-applies the scale after each install/upgrade (a full reinstall or k3s upgrade resets it to 1). No server flags changed.

⚠️ Note: the two CoreDNS replicas are not held apart by affinity (the embedded manifest has none), so they *could* co-locate. Traefik is properly anti-affined; CoreDNS is best-effort for now. If a drill ever shows them co-located, self-manage CoreDNS (`--disable coredns` + 2-replica chart in GitOps).
