## Step 5: point the agent at the gateway (completed 2026-10-22)
- `devcontainer.json`: removed `OPENROUTER_API_KEY` from `remoteEnv` (the last direct OpenRouter dependency).
- Gateway config lives in a project-level `opencode.json` at the repo root with `limit` caps per model (`context: 200000`, `output: 8000`), provider wired to `{env:LLM_GATEWAY_URL}` and `{env:LLM_GATEWAY_KEY}`.
- Verify passes: `OPENROUTER_API_KEY` empty inside the container, agent answers through the gateway.

### Step 5 lessons learned
- **remoteEnv vs containerEnv:** `devcontainer.json` `remoteEnv` didn't reach `docker exec` sessions (the agent harnesses). Switched to `containerEnv` which propagates to all container processes, not just the devcontainer shell.
- **History volume ownership:** The `opencode-data` volume for shell history was mounted root-owned and needed `chown` of `~/.local` in `postCreateCommand`. Without it, opencode couldn't write history.
- **OpenRouter balance guard:** OpenRouter rejected requests when the account balance couldn't cover full 32k-token replies (the default max_tokens in the provider). Adding `limit.output: 8000` in opencode.json keeps requests within balance while still providing ample headroom.
